# Client process, quotes, contracts and rights for ad work

Built 21 Nov 2026. Facts marked [V] were re-read on the source by a separate verifier on 21 Nov 2026; [D] = found in research, not re-read. NOT LEGAL ADVICE: a lawyer checks a contract once before first use. Industry documents quoted here are US / UK trade-body guidance, used as models, not Israeli standards.

## 1. Before quoting - questions to ask (AICP bidding guidance 2026 [D])
1. What is the budget, and is it "all in" (socials, stills, post, talent, music, insurance)?
2. Single bid or several bidders?
3. Exact deliverables: lengths, ratios, languages, versions.
4. Where and how long will it run (this sets usage).
5. Who approves, and by when.
Do not start work before a signed contract or purchase order [D].

## 2. Two ways to price a job (AICP live-action guidelines, revised 04/2020)
| Method | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Firm bid | The accepted proposal becomes the contract price unless the specifications change; no cost accounting afterwards [D] |
| Cost-plus-fixed-fee | Client pays actual direct costs plus a fixed fee [D] |
AICP publishes no standard mark-up percentage; each company sets its own [D]. No official Israeli price list for AI ads was found.

## 3. Payment, changes, cancellation - model terms [V]
| Term | Model wording (source) |
|---|---|
| Milestones, shoot-based job | 75% on signing, not later than 5 business days before the first shoot day; 25% on approval, not later than airing or 30 days from the final invoice (AICP "75-25 plan", shown as an example) |
| Milestones, post-production job | 75% on award and before work starts; 25% on delivery; invoices within 30 days (AICP, 21 Jan 2026) |
| Late payment (US model) | interest at Prime + 2% on payments later than 30 days (example) |
| Change of specification | an overage, agreed before the work; 75% of the overage due on signing it |
| Cancellation | 1-10 business days before the shoot or during it: all costs + full fees as bid; more than 10 business days: all costs + not less than 50% of the fees |
| Ownership | "Title of ownership of a commercial does not transfer until full payment is made" |
For Moshe (INFERENCE): for an AI job, "first shoot day" becomes the day generation starts; keep the same structure.
Israel: a business client pays not later than 45 days from the end of the month in which the invoice was delivered, unless the contract expressly sets another date (Supplier Payments Law; bill text official, current text read on a community mirror) [V].

## 4. Approvals and revision rounds
- Approve in writing, or comment in writing; client delay extends the schedule (AIGA standard agreement [D]).
- No trade body publishes a standard number of rounds. Community sources say two to three rounds is common [D] - write the number into the contract and name each round as it is used.
- For broadcast in the UK, legal text on screen has size and hold-time rules (Clearcast [V]): 0.2 seconds per word plus 2 seconds for up to 9 words or 3 seconds for 10 or more. Use only as a readability model outside the UK.

## 5. Who owns the film - Israel (Copyright Act 2007, WIPO English translation) [V]
1. s.35(a): in a commissioned work the first owner is the AUTHOR, unless the commissioner and the author agreed otherwise, expressly or impliedly. So the contract must say what the client gets.
2. s.37(b): an assignment or licence can be limited to a territory, a period or specific acts - this is the legal basis of usage terms.
3. s.37(c): an assignment of copyright or an EXCLUSIVE licence must be in a written document.
4. s.34: an employee's work belongs to the employer unless agreed otherwise (not Moshe's case as a freelancer).

## 6. Usage terms - the five dials (write all five in every quote)
| Dial | Options |
|---|---|
| Media | organic social / paid social / online video / TV / cinema / outdoor / all media |
| Territory | Israel / named countries / worldwide |
| Term | months or years from first airing / in perpetuity |
| Exclusivity | exclusive in a category / non-exclusive |
| Versions | which lengths and ratios are included; extra cut-downs priced separately |
Buyout = all media, worldwide, in perpetuity for one fee. Licence = limited by the dials; more use later = a renewal fee. Third-party parts (music, voices, stock, AI tools) keep their own licences and must cover the same dials.

## 7. AI-specific clauses (AICP AI guidelines for production contracts, file dated 21 Oct 2026; optional guidance) [V]
1. State in the agreement the use and purpose of generative AI, and any limits.
2. Prefer a platform that gives commercial terms with indemnity, does not train on inputs or outputs, and lets the advertiser use the output without restriction; if the client requires a platform that does not, get that acknowledged in writing.
3. The producer keeps ownership of inputs and prompts; the advertiser typically gets the final output.
4. Both sides acknowledge that AI-generated material "may not, in whole or in part, be subject to copyright or other intellectual property protections".
5. Producer promises commercially reasonable human review; final clearance stays with the agency / advertiser.
6. Indemnity only to the extent the AI platform itself provides one.
7. The client is responsible for disclosure duties (for example AI-generated humans) and for its own inputs.
8. The client may not use the deliverables to train AI.
9. Keep prompts and inputs archived in full.
10. Any likeness release is "for this project only and not in perpetuity".
US Copyright Office, 29 Jan 2025 [V]: AI output is protected only where a human determined sufficient expressive elements - "not the mere provision of prompts"; AI assistance does not bar copyright. No Israeli ruling on this was found. Meaning for Moshe (INFERENCE): assign "all rights that exist", and point to the human parts - script, edit, selection and arrangement.

## 8. Contract checklist (assemble from sections 1-7)
Parties and scope; deliverables table; schedule and what client delay does; price and what is included; milestones; revision rounds and price of extra rounds; change orders; cancellation; approvals in writing; ownership and the five usage dials; third-party licences list; AI clauses; releases; portfolio right; confidentiality; liability limit; governing law. Free model agreements to read: AIGA Standard Form of Agreement, Freelancers Union Contract Creator [D].

## Lessons learned
- 21 Nov 2026: under Israeli law the freelancer owns a commissioned film unless agreed otherwise, and an exclusive licence must be in writing - so every quote needs the usage dials.
