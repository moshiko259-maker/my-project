## 1. Who regulates what
| Channel | Rule set |
|---|---|
| Commercial TV (Keshet 12, Reshet 13, Channel 14) and regional radio | Second Authority for Television and Radio, under the Second Authority Law 1990 and its rules [REG] |
| Kan (public broadcaster) | Paid commercials allowed on RADIO; on TV only sponsorship notices and paid public-service spots; none on the children's channel or its website (Kan law s.69-74) [MIRROR] |
| Galei Tzahal / Galgalatz | Sponsorship broadcasts and service spots only, no regular commercials; no qualitative or comparative description; at most four and a half minutes per broadcast hour [MIRROR] |
| 2026 broadcasting reform law | Passed 16 Jul 2026 (53 to 48); the High Court froze the whole law on 29 Oct 2026 with an order nisi, so the 1990 law and its rules still apply [PRESS + MIRROR; the court decision itself was not read] |

## 2. Pre-approval of a TV commercial [REG, Pre-approval Rules 1994]
1. Not every ad needs prior approval: the Director may designate ads or types of ads that do (s.2). The current list of designated types is UNKNOWN - ask the channel.
2. The licensee (the channel) applies in writing no later than 7 days before the planned air date, with the script / storyboard or the ad (s.2א).
3. No decision by the deadline = the channel may air it, unless the Director announced an extension (s.3(ב)).
4. Appeal to the Council within 7 days; the advertiser and the ad agency may appeal too (s.3(ד)).
5. Any change after approval needs approval again (s.4(ב)); the Director may disqualify an ad that did not need approval (s.4א); the channel must check every ad itself (s.7).
Practical meaning for Moshe (INFERENCE): the channel submits, not the freelancer; give the client the storyboard and script early enough for the 7 days.

## 3. TV ethics rules - content [REG, Ethics in Television Advertising Rules 1994]
- s.3: no harm to human dignity, good taste or public feelings.
- s.9: no hidden, subliminal or incidental advertising.
- s.11: no message on a political, social, public or economic matter that is publicly controversial.
- s.12 banned: tobacco and smoking products; products meant to evade a legal test; mysticism and the occult; private investigation agencies; weapons, ammunition, shooting clubs; obscene material, escort and sex services.
- s.23 alcohol (2% or more by volume): not aimed at minors, no minor on screen, no claim that it contributes to health, success or sexual success.
- s.24: no misleading. s.26 comparisons: similar products, a uniform basis, facts that can be substantiated, presented fairly.
- s.27 price: the price shown is the full consumer price; a sale states the period it is valid.
- s.40-41 minors: no exploiting that minors are easier to persuade; no minors in an appearance unsuitable for minors.
- s.42-43 health: needs a suitable medical opinion; no prescription-only medicines; no cure claims. s.44-46 food: "healthy" needs a medical basis; vitamins not presented as essential.
- s.54א subtitles inside a commercial: contrasting colour, letter height not less than 16 pixels, on screen at least 5 consecutive seconds.

## 4. Time limits and scheduling [REG, Scheduling Rules 1992]
Max 10 minutes of ads per hour (between 20:00 and 24:00 free allocation if the total is not over 40 minutes); not over 10% of daily broadcast time; one commercial is at most 90 seconds; transition gap at least 3 seconds. Children: merchandise ads based on a children's programme not within two hours before and after it; an ad with a children's-programme character not during children's programmes or 30 minutes before and after.

## 5. Loudness and subtitles
- Loudness [REG, Loudness Rules 2009 s.2]: a TV programme, ad or promo not above Dialnorm minus 26 dB and not below minus 30 dB, average minus 28 dB. Radio: an ad not louder than the programme's range. This is NOT the European R128 figure. Whether channels apply these values in practice today is UNKNOWN - ask the channel's traffic desk.
- Subtitles [MIRROR, Television Broadcasts (Subtitles and Signing) Law 2005 s.3(ה)]: a TV broadcaster must accompany commercials with subtitles.

## 6. General ad law [MIRROR unless noted]
| Law | What the text says |
|---|---|
| Consumer Protection Law 1981 s.2 | No act or omission likely to mislead a consumer on any material matter |
| s.7 | Liable for a misleading ad: the one on whose behalf it was made AND the person who brought it to publication. An ad in the form of an article without a clear "advertisement" mark is deemed misleading |
| s.17ד | A price is shown as the total price only, in Israeli currency; "total" includes VAT and mandatory fees |
| s.7א + Regulations 1991 (ads aimed at minors; minor = under 18) | No misleading a minor or exploiting age, innocence or inexperience; no nudity or sexual hints; no "only" / "just" on a price unless a significant discount |
| Youth Labor Law s.4 | A child under 15 may not be employed in a performance, in advertising or in advertising photo shoots without a time-limited permit; never in school hours; whoever orders the shoot carries half the employer's penalty. s.4א: ages 15-18 need the youth's and a parent's consent on the statutory form |
| Alcohol Advertising Law 2012 [REG + MIRROR] | No alcohol ads on billboards; none in print mainly for under-18s; an ad may contain only the listed details (brand, maker, packaging, alcohol %, price and similar); warning text required |
| Tobacco Advertising Ban s.3 | No advertising of a smoking product (printed-newspaper exception to be deleted on 1.4.2029) |
| Weight Restriction in the Modelling Industry Law 2012 | A photographed ad with a model needs a doctor's certificate (within 3 months before the shoot; adult BMI not under 18.5); if editing narrowed body measurements, a clear notice on at least 7% of the ad area. The law defines an ad as a photographed ad; how it applies to AI-generated bodies is UNKNOWN |
| Protection of Privacy Law s.2(6) | Using a person's name, nickname, picture or voice for profit is an invasion of privacy |
| Roads (Signage) Law 1966 (NOT re-read by the verifier) | Signs visible from inter-urban roads need a permit; municipal by-laws and fees per city: UNKNOWN |
| AI-generated content in commercial ads | No binding Israeli labelling rule was found (Nov 2026); binding rules exist only for election propaganda. Absence of a finding is not proof |
