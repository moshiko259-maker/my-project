## 2. CONFLICTS
1. Loudness: the brief assumed EBU R128; the Second Authority and Kan rules state Dialnorm minus 28 dB average (range minus 26 to minus 30). No Israeli official R128 requirement found.
2. Allocation-number schedule: original 2024 path (15,000 in 2026, 10,000 in 2027, 5,000 from 2028 - shibolet.com, chamber.org.il) vs accelerated path (10,000 from 1.1.2026, 5,000 from 1.6.2026 - State Comptroller report, greeninvoice). The accelerated one is current.
3. Ethics rules "1994 and updates": the 2021 rules cover branded content only; the 1994 ethics rules remain the listed ad-ethics text, with deleted sub-sections.
4. Broadcast reform: law passed 16.7.2026 but suspended in full by the High Court since 29.10.2026.
